🔍 Analytical Observations & Findings

1. Tabular vs Deep RL

Existing agents are weak at converting KRvK

The saved 200-game benchmark recorded 2 checkmates each for Q-Learning and DQN (1%), and none for PPO. These results are poor; they motivate the tablebase-supervised Dueling Double-DQN experiment rather than proving that the new model is stronger.

2. Value vs Policy Methods

Keep comparisons protocol-aware

Training episode metrics, held-out tablebase move accuracy, and game win rates measure different things. The checkpoint comparison reports these separately; no model is called strong until it has been evaluated on the same fixed positions and defender protocol.

3. Reward Shaping Impact

Supervised warm start is not proof of RL improvement

The warm-start model imitates exact tablebase labels. Optional Double-DQN fine-tuning starts from that checkpoint and can improve or degrade it. We compare both versions independently and retain the distinction in the results table.

♟️ Problem Definition: King + Rook vs King (KRK)

The King and Rook versus lone King endgame is an elementary chess endgame with a guaranteed theoretical win for the side with the rook within at most 16 moves from any legal position. Despite this theoretical certainty, discovering the mating technique through trial and error represents a rigorous multi-step reinforcement learning challenge requiring coordinated piece movement.

🔲

State Space $\mathcal{S}$

Represented in two distinct paradigms:

  • Tabular Indexing: Compact integer representation $S = s_{wk} \cdot 64^2 + s_{wr} \cdot 64 + s_{bk}$, spanning exactly $64^3 = 262,144$ state entries.
  • Neural Tensor: 193-dimensional feature vector containing 3 one-hot planes ($3 \times 64 = 192$ bits) indicating White King, White Rook, and Black King coordinates, concatenated with a normalized move-counter scalar $\frac{t}{100}$.
🎮

Action Space $\mathcal{A}$

36 discrete action indices covering all permissible White piece transitions:

  • King Moves (8): 1 square in all 8 cardinal & diagonal directions.
  • Rook Moves (28): 4 orthogonal directions $\times$ 7 maximal step distances.
  • Action Masking: An active binary boolean mask $\mathcal{M}(s) \in \{0, 1\}^{36}$ filters out illegal moves, checks, and square collisions prior to $\text{argmax}$ or softmax sampling.
🏆

Reward Function $\mathcal{R}(s, a, s')$

Carefully balanced reward shaping balancing sparse goals with dense guidance:

Terminal Checkmate+10.0 + (100 - t) × 0.05
Terminal Stalemate-5.0
Rook Captured (Draw)-8.0
Check Delivered+0.30
Edge Restriction ($\Delta d_{\text{center}}$)+0.15 × progress
King Proximity ($\Delta d_{\text{kings}}$)+0.05 × progress
Time Decay / Move Step-0.02
⏱️

50-Move Rule Constraint

Under FIDE rules, if 50 consecutive full moves (100 half-moves) elapse without a capture or pawn move, the game is declared an immediate Draw. This imposes a strict finite horizon $T=100$ where failure to checkmate results in an episode timeout, penalizing stalled policies.

🧠 Algorithmic Architectures Compared

Tabular RL

Q-Learning

$$Q(s, a) \leftarrow Q(s, a) + \alpha \left[ r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s', a') - Q(s, a) \right]$$

Classic model-free off-policy control using a memory-efficient sparse hash table. Exploits greedy selection with exponential decay $\epsilon$-greedy exploration ($\epsilon_0=1.0 \to \epsilon_{\min}=0.05$). Guaranteed asymptotic convergence to the Bellman optimality equation in finite MDPs.

Value Approximation

Deep Q-Network (DQN)

$$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \left( r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s', a'; \theta^-) - Q(s, a; \theta) \right)^2 \right]$$

Feedforward deep neural architecture ($193 \to 256 \to 128 \to 36$) parameterized by weights $\theta$. Incorporates an Experience Replay Buffer (capacity 50,000 transitions) and periodic Target Network updates ($\tau=1000$ steps) to break temporal correlation and stabilize gradient variance.

Policy Gradient

Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)

$$L^{CLIP}(\theta) = \hat{\mathbb{E}}_t \left[ \min\left( r_t(\theta)\hat{A}_t, \, \text{clip}(r_t(\theta), 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon)\hat{A}_t \right) \right]$$

Actor-Critic architecture sharing a 128-unit representation layer, outputting both policy logits $\pi_\theta(a|s)$ and state value estimates $V_\phi(s)$. Utilizes Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE, $\lambda=0.95$), mini-batch stochastic gradient descent, and entropy regularization.
